The 8 common blood groups

Blood group classification is based on inherited markers such as ABO and Rh systems.

ABO + Rh
A+
A Positive A antigen with Rh-positive status.
A−
A Negative A antigen with Rh-negative status.
B+
B Positive B antigen with Rh-positive status.
B−
B Negative B antigen with Rh-negative status.
AB+
AB Positive Both A and B antigens with Rh-positive status.
AB−
AB Negative Both A and B antigens with Rh-negative status.
O+
O Positive No A/B antigens with Rh-positive status.
O−
O Negative No A/B antigens with Rh-negative status.

What is blood made of?

Blood contains different components with different biological roles.

BASICS
●
Red Blood Cells

Carry oxygen from the lungs to tissues and help transport carbon dioxide back.

✦
White Blood Cells

Participate in the body's immune and defence responses.

◆
Platelets

Help the body form clots and control bleeding.

◌
Plasma

The liquid component that carries cells, proteins and other substances.

+
Proteins

Plasma contains proteins with important transport and regulatory functions.

≋
Other Substances

Blood also transports nutrients, hormones, electrolytes and metabolic waste.
