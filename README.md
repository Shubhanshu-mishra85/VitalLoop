# Vital Loop

### Connecting People. Supporting Care.

Vital Loop is a patient-centric healthcare coordination platform designed to help people discover potential blood resources, coordinate with donor networks and authorised healthcare facilities, and track emergency requests through one structured journey.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

During a blood emergency, people may struggle to:

- Find potential blood resources quickly
- Identify nearby healthcare facilities
- Coordinate with potential donors
- Share emergency requirements clearly
- Track the progress of a request
- Understand what action to take next

Vital Loop brings these steps into one connected digital experience.

---

## 💡 The Solution

Vital Loop creates a structured coordination loop:

```text
Emergency Request
        ↓
Requirement Analysis
        ↓
Smart Matching
        ↓
Resource Discovery
        ↓
Verification
        ↓
Coordination
        ↓
Status Tracking
        ↓
Resolution / Closure
