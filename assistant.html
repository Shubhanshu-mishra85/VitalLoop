⚠ Responsible AI

VitaAI provides healthcare navigation and coordination support. It does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medicines, determine clinical blood compatibility, guarantee blood availability, or replace doctors, pharmacists, blood-centre staff or emergency services. Actual blood collection, testing, storage, compatibility decisions, release and transfusion remain with authorised healthcare professionals and facilities.
